# Merged Sources on Cognitive Biases, Information Overload, Recommender Systems, and Decision Support

## 1. Information overload, cognitive biases, and bounded rationality (general)

- "Cognitive Biases in Digital Decision-Making: How Consumers Navigate Information Overload" (Advances in Consumer Research, 2025).[^1][^2]
- "Strategic and Managerial Decision-Making for Sustainable Management: Factors and Remedies for Information Overload" (book chapter / monograph).[^3][^4]
- "Consumer Anxiety in the Digital Age: Analyzing the Impact of Information Overload on Decision-Making" (conference paper, 2024).[^5]
- "Antecedents and Consequences of Information Overload in the COVID-19 Pandemic" (risk and health communication study).[^6]
- "Information overload for (bounded) rational agents" (Pothos et al., theoretical and simulation analysis).[^7][^8]
- "How Does Information Overload Affect Consumers' Online Decision Process? An Event-Related Potentials Study" (ERP study on online purchasing).[^9][^10]
- "Information overload in the information age: a review of the literature" (Roetzel, pre‑2020 but heavily cited in recent work).[^11]
- "Information overload in decision making: a systematic review of the literature" (Hartmann dissertation and related articles on accounting‑based decision‑making under information overload).[^12][^13]
- "Dealing with information overload: a comprehensive review" (workplace/organizational review, Bundesanstalt für Arbeitsschutz und Arbeitsmedizin).[^14][^15][^16]
- "Strategies for managing information overload: a systematic review" (Emerald, 2025/2026).[^17][^18]
- "Causes, consequences, and strategies to deal with information overload: A scoping review" (multidomain scoping review, 2024).[^19]
- "Information overload research in accounting: a systematic review" (Managerial Review and related outlets).[^20][^21]
- "Navigating Information Overload: Strategies and Insights" (Frontiers in Psychology article on strategies and cognitive mechanisms).[^22]
- "Information Overload" (The Decision Lab explainer and evidence summary).[^23]
- "Choice Overload Bias" (The Decision Lab explainer on paradox of choice and overload).[^24]
- "Bias Busters: How cognitive overload multiplies every bias" (McKinsey article, 2026).[^25]
- "The urgent need to address information overload in the digital era" (blog/article summarizing recent evidence and risks).[^26]

## 2. Information overload, interpretability, and digital decision environments

- "Interpretability Gone Bad: The Role of Bounded Rationality" (Kaur et al., CSCW‑style paper on interpretability tools and bounded rationality).[^27]
- "Dealing with information overload: a comprehensive review" (duplicate listings capturing different publication contexts and access points).[^15][^16][^14]
- "Strategies for managing information overload: a systematic review" (organization‑ and ICT‑level strategies to cope with overload).[^18][^17]
- "The urgent need to address information overload in the digital era" (higher‑level reflection on digital society and overload).[^26]

## 3. Cognitive biases and information overload in accounting and managerial decision-making

- Dissertation: "Accounting-Based Decision-Making under Information Overload" (systematic review plus experiments on accounting‑based decisions).[^13]
- "Information overload research in accounting: a systematic review" (Management Review, 2024).[^21][^20]
- "Strategic and Managerial Decision-Making for Sustainable Management: Factors and Remedies for Information Overload" (managerial perspective).[^4][^3]

## 4. Cognitive biases in recommender systems (anchoring, confirmation/filter bubbles, choice overload)

- "Anchoring effects of recommender systems" (early ACM paper quantifying anchoring on predicted ratings).[^28]
- "Do Recommender Systems Manipulate Consumer Preferences? A Study of Anchoring Effects" (INFORMS / Information Systems Research article and its earlier working paper versions).[^29][^30][^31][^32]
- "Recommender Systems, Consumer Preferences, and Anchoring" (workshop/short paper variant on anchoring).[^32]
- "Filter Bubbles in Recommender Systems: Fact or Fallacy — A Systematic Review" (systematic review of empirical and simulation work, 2024).[^33][^34][^35][^36]
- "Deconstructing the Filter Bubble: User Decision-Making and Recommender Systems" (modeling study on user choice and RS feedback loops).[^37]
- "Choice overload and recommendation effectiveness in related-article recommendations: Analyzing the Sowiport digital library" (empirical study of related‑article recommendation list size).[^38]

## 5. Choice overload and the paradox of choice in recommender and online settings

- "The Choice Overload Effect in Online Recommender Systems" (large‑scale field experiment on recommendation set size and purchase probability; working paper and final journal versions).[^39][^40][^41][^42]
- "Decisions with ChatGPT: Reexamining choice overload in AI‑mediated recommendations" (study on option set size, perceived overload, and satisfaction in LLM‑based recommendations).[^43][^44]
- "Recommender systems: Friend (of choice) or foe? A large-scale field experiment" (field study on curated shortlists vs larger sets).[^45]
- "Examining Choice Overload across Single-list and Multi-list User Interfaces" (study on carousel/multi‑list layouts and overload).[^46][^47]
- "Does the paradox of choice exist in theory? A behavioral search model and Pareto-improving choice set reduction algorithm" (theoretical work on search costs and optimal choice‑set reduction).[^48]
- "GEN Z AND THE PROBLEM OF PARADOX OF CHOICE" (survey/empirical work on young users and choice overload).[^49]
- "As Few as Three Options Can Be Too Many for Online Shoppers" (popular summary of academic findings on small‑set overload).[^50]
- "THE PARADOX OF CHOICE" (IRJMETS article reviewing paradox‑of‑choice literature).[^51]
- "The Paradox of Choice: Why More Options Produce Worse Decisions" (blog/summary article).[^52]
- "How Recommendation Algorithms Solve the Paradox of Choice" (LinkedIn article discussing RS as paradox‑of‑choice mitigators).[^53]

## 6. Decision support systems, intelligent advisors, and bounded rationality

- "Bounded Rationality and Cognitive Bias: A Meta-Synthetic Study" (overview of bounded rationality and bias in modern decision environments).[^54][^55]
- "Bounded rationality" (Wikipedia entry summarizing the foundational concept and related models).[^56]
- CESifo Working Paper No. 8341 (on bounded rationality / decision behavior under information constraints).[^57]
- "Foresight as decision-making support within bounded rationality in individuals and organizations – Embrapa’s strategic intelligence system – Agropensa’s case" (case study of strategic intelligence as DSS).[^58]
- Dissertation: "Decision biases in user agreement with intelligent decision aids" (Solomon; experimental work on agreement, trust, and bias in intelligent decision aids).[^59][^60]

## 7. Patient decision aids and bias / preference sensitivity

- "Features of Computer-Based Decision Aids: Systematic Review" (JMIR review of design features and their impact on decision quality).[^61]
- "Decision aids to prepare patients for shared decision making: Two randomized controlled experiments on the impact of awareness of preference-sensitivity and personal motives".[^62]
- "Testing Whether Decision Aids Introduce Cognitive Biases: Results of a Randomized Trial" (trial on ordering/context effects and debiasing in risk communication).[^63]

## 8. Conversational and intelligent recommenders for preference articulation

- "Dialogue Strategies for Conversational Recommender Systems" (early work on dialogue‑based preference elicitation).[^64]
- "Decision-Making in Recommender Systems" (position/overview paper on decision‑making aspects in RS).[^65]
- "Variational Reasoning about User Preferences for Conversational Recommendation" (variational user modeling for conversational RS).[^66]
- "Conversational Recommender Systems based on Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs" (LLM‑enhanced conversational RS design).[^67]
- "RecUserSim: A Realistic and Diverse User Simulator for Evaluating Recommender Systems" (user simulator to study decision and interaction patterns).[^68]

## 9. High-level syntheses and general explainers

- "Information Overload" (The Decision Lab) and "Choice Overload Bias" entries summarizing psychological mechanisms and experiments.[^23][^24]
- "Bias Busters: How cognitive overload multiplies every bias" (McKinsey article on overload and cognitive bias in organizations).[^25]
- "The urgent need to address information overload in the digital era" (overview of societal and organizational challenges).[^26]
- Popular explainers on paradox of choice and recommendation algorithms as mitigators.[^52][^50][^53]
